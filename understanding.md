# Understanding — 0018 Oddments Site

Accumulated tacit knowledge. Not decisions, not architecture — the feel of the project, the sensibility behind the choices, the things that shaped the work without producing an artifact. Added to and refined in place across SC rotations. Read this from source, do not paraphrase it into a handoff.

---

## What Oddments is, in a sentence Pablo would recognise

A side brand and label for his personal design projects, with the intent to productise them. Oddkit and Oddscale and Oddfaces sit under it. It is not Raw Materials work and it is not his personal practice — it is the shelf the products go on.

## The mark carries more meaning than its size suggests

●◗◗ is a Unicode string chosen so the logotype survives as plain text anywhere. That is a real constraint and a real idea: the mark is typeable. It also reads ODD, and *odd* means not divisible by two, which is live conceptual material rather than a coincidence. Ideas that come from that material land; ideas applied on top of the shapes do not.

## The design bar, learned the hard way

The first round of animation options — reveal, phase, breathe, wave — were rejected outright: *"these are so meh. boring."* What was wrong with them was not craft. It was that each was a technique applied to the mark rather than an idea coming out of it. For a mark of three primitives, technique has nowhere to hide.

The direction that stuck came from Pablo, not from the option list. The lesson to carry: on this project, propose ideas with a premise, not effects with an easing curve. If a proposal could be applied to any logo, it is the wrong proposal.

## Precision is the aesthetic

Pablo caught a spacing bug (half-discs given full-circle advance widths) unprompted, and then set the gap himself at 2 with zero optical trim — very tight, denser than most people would choose. The mark should read as one compact block. Sloppiness in the geometry is the failure mode this project is most exposed to, because at this level of reduction there is nothing else to look at.

He works in aesthetic vocabulary and types fast. "Meh" and "boring" are complete and sufficient feedback; do not ask him to justify them.

## The joke worth protecting

ODD is the only state where the mark is legible, and the three city buttons are states where it isn't. The mark spells itself only when it stops telling you anything useful. That's quietly funny and it is the piece's whole personality. Anything that makes the clock more useful at the expense of that tension is probably the wrong trade.

The LDN / NYC / SPO set also maps onto British, American and Brazilian. Pablo is British-Brazilian and lives in New York. He did not say this was why; a viewer will read it as deliberate either way.

## Where the piece is fragile

- **Numerals below ~200px** fall apart. This forces the clock to be the page rather than a small mark in a corner. If a future SC wants the mark small, the numerals drop and only 12/3/6/9 survive.
- **The red** is the only colour in an otherwise monochrome system. It is not yet decided whether it is the Oddments accent or a foreign object that happened to arrive with the seconds hand. Treat it as unresolved.
- **Draw order** of the seconds hand relative to the hands and the numeral ring was inherited from build order, not chosen.

## A correction recorded so it is not re-inherited

SC I stated during the originating conversation that the two hands occlude each other when they overlap. They do not — they sit at different orbital radii. The claim was fluent and wrong, and it briefly framed the coincidence as a problem when it is the payoff. Check the geometry before describing the behaviour.
